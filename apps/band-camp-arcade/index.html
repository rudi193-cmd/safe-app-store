Band Camp Arcade

Six small, silly, entirely local things for marching band kids. No accounts, no network calls — everything stays in this browser tab.

Tuning Note Purgatory

Hit start. A concert Bb plays forever. See how long you last before you hit stop.

Pit Crew Simulator

Drag every front-ensemble instrument onto its spot before the count runs out. Miss and you retry.

Uniform Sweat Tracker

Log the misery of every game and competition. Crowns a champion of suffering at season's end.

Sectional Bingo

A shuffled bingo card of stock sectional disasters. Add your own inside jokes to the pool.

GE Score Roast

Feed it a fictional score and get a dramatically petty imaginary judge's comment back. Satire only.

Drum Major Says

Simon, in shako. Watch the calls, repeat them back, one more each round until you break formation.
