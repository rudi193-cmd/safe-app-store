# Hero ASCII art sources

- **FREEDOM 250 word mark** — original half-block lettering, this project
- **Star field** — original, seeded scatter (`tui_art.hero_field`)
- **CLI / banner Liberty Bell** — Joan G. Stark (jgs / Spunk), *Liberty Bell* (7/97), from the July 4th collection on rec.arts.ascii / alt.ascii-art tradition

  - Archive: https://oldcompcz.github.io/jgs/joan_stark/july4.html
  - Mirror: Christopher Johnson's ASCII Art Collection, artwork #1711 — https://asciiart.website/art/1711

  Used verbatim in `bell.py` (`LIBERTY_BELL` / `LIBERTY_BELL_PLAIN`). The comma on the bell face is the crack — part of the original piece.
